About this site

Mortal Shell 2 Guide is an independent English-language reference for walkthrough routes, locations, and completion tracking.

What we publish

We focus on clear main-story walkthroughs and separate location guides. A collectible page should answer where an item is, what is required, and what the player should see on the way there.

How it is made

Final route pages are based on original gameplay captures and current-build checks. External sources may be used to identify leads, but their images are not republished here.

Unofficial status

This is not an official site and is not affiliated with Cold Symmetry, Playstack, Steam, Sony, or any platform holder. Game names and marks belong to their respective owners.
