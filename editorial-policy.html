Editorial standards

Editorial policy

Accuracy matters more than publishing a large list quickly.

Verification labels

  • Verified route: played and captured in the stated game version.
  • Reference entry: a research lead that still needs an in-game capture pass.
  • Under review: a report with conflicting sources, an unclear requirement, or a possible patch change.

Images and maps

Published route images and annotations are original gameplay captures. We do not copy screenshots, maps, or videos from other guide sites.

Corrections

When a reader report is reproducible, we update the route and add the applicable version or correction note. Game updates can change balance, requirements, and locations; older pages are reviewed rather than silently treated as current.

Build advice

We explain mechanics and requirements, not a single “correct” boss strategy. Readers can choose the Shell, weapon, and approach that suits them.
