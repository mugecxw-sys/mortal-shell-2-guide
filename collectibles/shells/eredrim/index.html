How to get
Eredrim

Start from Citadel of Penance Beacon, take the upper route into the Citadel, then clear the final enemy encounter to reveal the Shell.

Eredrim

Verified game terms: Eredrim's active ability is Shoulder Bash, which deals Break Damage. His passive, Executioner, executes low-health enemies and gains Slaughterer stacks through Ripostes. Ethereal Diapason is his secondary Break-Damage shockwave. This page covers acquisition only.
