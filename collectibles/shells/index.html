Collectibles / Shells

All Shell Locations

Find all eight selectable Shells with original, annotated acquisition routes. Harros is also listed separately because he is the prologue-only starting Shell.

Route status: every selectable Shell now has a captured unlock route. Each guide names the starting Beacon, required encounter or item, and the final interaction.

01

Harros

Prologue only

Stone Stun: petrifies nearby enemies; hardened targets take Break Damage instead of normal damage. Role: balanced prologue tutorial Shell. Reported as lost at the end of the prologue.

02

Tiel

Fainweald

Lingering Shadow: enter Shadow, then use a light attack for a Shadow Strike. Shadow Dash: a last-second dodge grants Shadow and adds Stagger Damage. Role: stealth, timing, and burst windows. Get the annotated Tiel route in Widow’s Overlook.

03

Proxima

Fainweald

Biosampler: a hook pulls a target—or Proxima—into range, followed by Lightning. Grafted Armor: a chance to reduce melee or ranged damage and prevent stagger. Role: durable close-range control. Get the annotated Proxima route at Blackridge Cliffs.

04

Gragu

Fainweald

Staggering Blow: charge a heavy punch for stronger damage and heavy stagger. Revered Heart: restores health and can be replenished by defeating enemies. Role: sustain and stagger pressure. Get the annotated Gragu route from the One-Legged Wolf Tavern.

05

Eredrim

Fainweald

Shoulder Bash / Ethereal Diapason: break-focused shoulder charge and area shockwave. Executioner: can execute low-health enemies and builds Riposte-linked stacks. Role: aggressive Break Damage. Get the annotated Eredrim route from Citadel of Penance Beacon.

06

Smert

Fainweald

Miracle: pauses time; unarmed strikes apply Chaos that detonates when time resumes, with an optional health-cost extension. Deadly Revelation: a low-health Faith state. Role: high-risk burst setup. Get the annotated Smert route from Outskirts of Nochte Beacon.

07

Lazlo

Mammon

Retribution: heats armor for a shockwave; repeated use can trigger a stronger Burn-inflicting blast while disabling armor temporarily. Fortified Plate: damage reduction while armored. Role: armored front-line area damage. Get the annotated Lazlo route from High Lord's Courtyard Beacon.

08

Sariel

Mammon

Exodus of Thorns: sends out homing thorns that build Curse, at the cost of Pain. Purge: redirects part of incoming damage into Pain; maximum Pain improves dodge speed. Role: curse damage with risk management. Get the annotated Sariel route from The Silent Steps Beacon.

09

Genessa

Mammon

Faithful Doubles / Stray Doubles: summons attack copies; the Stray form can copy weapon and sidearm attacks for a duration. Duality: at zero health, becomes Stray rather than immediately separating from the Harbinger. Role: summon pressure and a second-life mechanic. Get the annotated Genessa route through Revenant Graves.

Original acquisition routes and gameplay captures last reviewed 29 August 2026. Ability terminology was cross-checked against the Probonk Shell catalogue and ability list.
