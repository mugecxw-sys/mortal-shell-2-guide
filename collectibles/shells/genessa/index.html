Shell acquisition · Mammon

How to get
Genessa

From Abbey Entrance Beacon, reach Revenant Graves, complete Sester's Censer, then return to Marrow Keep and offer it to Genessa.

Region
Mammon
Starting point
Abbey Entrance Beacon
Key item
Sester's Censer

Verified game terms: Genessa's active ability is Faithful Doubles, which summons copies for a weapon attack. Her passive is Duality, turning her into Stray rather than severing her when health is depleted. This page covers acquisition only.
