Map Fragments

There are 11 Map Fragments in Mortal Shell 2: five Map of Fainweald pieces and six Map of Mammon pieces. Follow each original annotated route from a named Beacon to reveal the full map and unlock Sat Nav.

Region one

Map of Fainweald

Five fragments are spread between Widow's Overlook, Mushroom Village and the outskirts around Nochte. Fainweald [5] requires the corruption-cleansing progression described below; the gate to Fainweald [2] requires the Citadel Gate Lever.

Map of Fainweald [3]

Start at Widow's Overlook Beacon.

Map of Fainweald [4]

Start at Widow's Overlook Beacon; the route passes the Mushroom Village gate.

Map of Fainweald [5]

Start at Mushroom Village Beacon.

Requirement: collect enough Ova and use the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep to unlock Mether's Breath and cleanse the corruption before taking this route.

Map of Fainweald [1]

Start at Widow's Overlook Beacon, then travel to Outskirts of Nochte Beacon.

Map of Fainweald [2]

Start at Outskirts of Nochte Beacon.

Requirement: obtain the Citadel Gate Lever. It opens the gate shown in the route before the final approach.

Region two

Map of Mammon

Six fragments are reached from Outskirts of Mammon Beacon and Castigator's Keep Beacon. The route images identify the correct portals, bridges and upper paths.

Map of Mammon [5]

Start at Outskirts of Mammon Beacon.

Map of Mammon [4]

Start at Outskirts of Mammon Beacon.

Map of Mammon [6]

Start at Outskirts of Mammon Beacon; this route passes the Map of Mammon [5] pickup.

Map of Mammon [3]

Start at Castigator's Keep Beacon.

Map of Mammon [2]

Start at Castigator's Keep Beacon.

Map of Mammon [1]

Start at Outskirts of Mammon Beacon.

Completion note: these 11 entries correspond to the five Map of Fainweald fragments and six Map of Mammon fragments needed for the Sat Nav achievement.
