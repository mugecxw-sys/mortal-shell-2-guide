Collectibles / sidearms

All sidearms

Eight sidearms are reported. This page holds the current name, requirement, and function notes until each route is replayed and captured.

Reference status: external sources report that all eight are collectible and none are missable. This will be retested before being presented as a completion guarantee.

01

Naylshotte

Prologue

Given by the Undermether in the prologue. Reported function: shotgun-like projectile spread.

02

Troubadour’s Lute

Fainweald

On the stage in the One-Legged Wolf Tavern. Reported requirement: Mother’s Breath. Reported function: inflicts Confusion; cannot be upgraded.

03

Forgotten Crossbow

Fainweald

Behind a locked door in the Flooded Village dungeon. The Damp Key is reported inside the village. Reported role: long-range shots.

04

Salvaged Trebuchaxe

Mammon

At the end of the Ravaged Hideout dungeon. Reported role: fires axes; holding fire charges the shot.

05

Triarch Repeater

Mammon

Reported in the final area of Blackwell Cavern, reached from north of Castigator’s Keep.

06

Cursed Child

Mammon

In the Revered Beacon dungeon near Abbey Entrance. Reported acquisition: destroy the dungeon’s light source.

07

Ballistazooka

Mammon

Reported reward for defeating the Subjugated Guardian in Sentry’s Grave.

08

Caged Hystrix

Mammon

Reported post-boss pickup in the Chamber of Becoming after Sariel, the Endless.

Reference source: All Things How sidearm location index. Last structured: 23 August 2026.
