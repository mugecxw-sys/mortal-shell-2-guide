All Sidearm Locations

Find all eight sidearms: seven pages use original annotated acquisition routes, while Naylshotte has a separate automatic-unlock guide.

Complete sidearm set: use the starting Beacon and pickup notes below for each unlock. Building a full loadout? Pair them with our primary weapon location guides.

01

Naylshotte

Prologue

Added automatically after the Prologue. No separate route or pickup is required. Open the annotated guide.

02

Troubadour's Lute

Fainweald

From One-Legged Wolf Beacon, enter the Tavern and take it from the marked stage. Open the annotated guide.

03

Forgotten Crossbow

Fainweald

Find the Damp Key in Flooded Village, then use it on the upper-floor door. Open the annotated guide.

04

Salvaged Trebuchaxe

Mammon

At Ravaged Hideout, defeat the Bloodcursed Lithopod to open the rock fissure, then inspect the corpse. Open the annotated guide.

05

Triarch Repeater

Mammon

Ride the right-side lift from Castigator’s Keep, then follow the route into Blackwell Cavern. Open the annotated guide.

06

Cursed Child

Mammon

From Sester’s Gate, reach Revered Beacon and destroy the glowing objective to reveal it. Open the annotated guide.

07

Ballistazooka

Mammon

Follow the Lonesome Spire route from Gate of Mammon Beacon and clear Sentry’s Grave. Open the annotated guide.

08

Caged Hystrix

Mammon

From The Silent Steps, follow the portal route to Chamber of Becoming and complete the Sariel sequence. Open the annotated guide.
