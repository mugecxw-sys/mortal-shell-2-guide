Fastest clean completion route

Unlock the full roster

Collect Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Lazlo, Sariel and Genessa. Harros is the prologue Shell and does not replace one of these eight sets. Use the linked location routes above to close any empty slot.

Finish one Shell at a time

Equip a Shell, spend Glimpses with Zhirelle in the Shellkeeper’s Vault until Bond Tier IV, then open its Memory list. Play all five entries and tick them here before changing Shells.

Confirm playback, not purchase

Let each scene finish and wait until normal control or the Shellkeeper menu returns. If dialogue overlaps or the next Memory does not appear, leave the conversation and speak to Zhirelle again.

Recycle or farm the shortfall

Use Mether’s Severance only after recording all five watched Memories for that Shell, or collect renewable Glimpse Stones and break them through enemy kills.

Current Glimpse farming route

Launch guides that call Glimpses finite predate the Week 1 Update. The official notes state that Glimpses and Tarcores are no longer finite and that the new Glimpse Stone wears down through enemy defeats before breaking into Glimpses.

  1. Progress far enough to open The Unfound Path and its Beacons.
  2. Use the Chamber of the Forsaken route and kill the Lithopod-like wooden crabs around the area. Rest at the Beacon and repeat for Glimpse Stone drops.
  3. Equip one Glimpse Stone. Community tests currently report that it breaks after 20 enemy kills and awards 3 Glimpses.
  4. Break it on a short, repeatable group: the bats near Blackridge Pass or the sheep near Sunken Village are current community routes. Rest, re-equip the next Stone, and repeat.
Route status: renewable Glimpses and the Glimpse Stone are official Week 1 features. Chamber of the Forsaken drops, the 20-kill/3-Glimpse value, and the bat or sheep loops are community-observed farming details and may change with balance patches.

How to use Mether’s Severance

Give Mether’s Severance to Zhirelle to sever the active bond, reset that Shell and recover all Glimpses spent on it. This is useful when your earlier upgrades are spread across several incomplete Shells.

  1. Max the current Shell and play all five Memories.
  2. Check its five boxes here and verify its in-game Memory list.
  3. Use Mether’s Severance with Zhirelle and move the refunded pool to the next Shell.
  4. Leave Genessa until late if your build cannot offer her the item; current players report that the option can be unavailable for Genessa/Stray Sester on some builds.

Community reports say already viewed Memories remain available after a reset and can contribute to the achievement, but the official patch note only promises the bond reset and Glimpse refund. Keep a save backup and verify your platform counter before repeating the method across the roster.

Community replay order

There is no official repair sequence. Recent players most often suggest replaying Genessa when the counter is exactly 35/40. If that does not move the counter, a reported wider order is Tiel → Proxima → Gragu → Smert → Eredrim → Lazlo → Sariel, checking progress after each complete five-Memory set. Other reports remain stuck after multiple replays, so this is a troubleshooting attempt rather than a guaranteed fix.

Do not overwrite your only completion save

Before severing bonds, starting NG+, restoring cloud data or repeating large groups of Memories, preserve the current save using your platform’s supported backup method. No community workaround should be treated as an official trophy repair.

Seeking the Past FAQ

Do I need 40 different Shells or pickups?

No. The total is five story Memories for each of eight selectable Shells.

Does maximum Bond automatically count all five?

No. Max Bond unlocks access, but Seeking the Past asks you to watch the Memories. Open the list and play each one.

Is NG++ still required?

No for Glimpse supply on an updated build. Week 1 made the resource renewable, allowing all Shells to be upgraded in one playthrough. NG+ remains an optional recovery route for an existing save or unresolved counter issue.

Is replaying Genessa an official fix for 35/40?

No. It is a community-reported workaround based on the pattern of one missing five-Memory set.
