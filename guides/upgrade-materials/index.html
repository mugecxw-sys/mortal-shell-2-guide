Upgrade Materials
& Tarforge Costs

Mortal Shell 2 uses five smithing materials to enhance primary weapons and sidearms at the Tarforge: Ventrium, Laterite, Dorsalite, Thoracium and Ossinite. Each covers five levels of the standard enhancement ladder.

Quick answer · +0 → +25

31 of each material. 185,650 Coin.

One primary weapon or sidearm needs 155 smithing materials across the complete standard ladder: 31 Ventrium, 31 Laterite, 31 Dorsalite, 31 Thoracium and 31 Ossinite. Coin totals cover enhancement fees only, not the cost of buying materials.

+25 is not an absolute maximum: the Endless Core removes the weapon and sidearm level cap. Costs beyond +25 are outside this guide.

Where to get upgrade materials

Use fixed pickups and finite vendor stock to finish a tier. The Merchant material shelf opens once you hold at least 16 Ova; shop availability still depends on your route and current game state.

All 5 Tarforge upgrades and locations

The Tarforge is in Marrow Keep. Give the first four tools to Franz; install the Endless Core through the Tarforge menu. The route summaries below were cross-checked against two independent location guides and the item database.

Primary weapons · Prologue

Muradean Actuator

Inside the Village Outskirts Beacon cleanse dungeon in Disciple’s Path. Open the chest before the elevator section, then bring the Actuator to Franz to enable primary-weapon enhancement. If you skip the Prologue, the item database says you begin with one.

Item reference →

Sidearms · Fainweald

Obsidian Lathe

From Widow’s Overlook, travel southeast to Martyr’s Tomb. Clear the dungeon to its final room and open the priest-guarded chest beside the sarcophagus. Bring the Lathe to Franz to enable sidearm enhancement.

Item reference →

Tarstones · Glutted Mire

Etching Needles

From Sunken Village, continue through the Glutted Mire beyond the Tarblighted Shepherd. Cross the wooden route toward Ruk; the chest below him, before the Magdalena encounter, contains the Needles. Give them to Franz to unlock Tarstone tempering.

Item reference →

Smelting · Mammon

Foundry Stone

Start at Outskirts of Mammon Beacon and head south down the battlefield toward the castle-wall fortifications. Open the chest near the enemies by the wall, then return the Stone to Franz to unlock equipment smelting.

Item reference →

Level cap · The Unfound Path

Endless Core

Enter the Hidden Nave Beacon cleanse dungeon. Reach the second bonfire, double back for the torch at the first bonfire, then carry it into the fog and follow the revealed route to the chest at the bottom. Install the Core at the Tarforge to remove the weapon and sidearm level cap.

Recommended spending order

  1. Build around one primary weapon first. Avoid dividing scarce early materials across weapons you have not tested.
  2. Try a sidearm before committing. Choose one that complements your preferred range and playstyle.
  3. Save the rare tiers for a settled loadout. Thoracium and Ossinite have fewer fixed records than the earlier materials.

The Foundry Stone unlocks equipment smelting, which recovers smithing materials from enhanced weapons and sidearms. Do not assume it refunds Coin or confuse it with Tarstone tempering.
